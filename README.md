## What is DefaultBrowserGuard?

As a Mac user who avoids Safari and prefers **Betterbird** or **Thunderbird** over Apple Mail, you know the drill: Regularly — at the latest after the next restart — macOS secretly resets your default apps to Apple's products. **Without asking. Without warning.**

**DefaultBrowserGuard** automatically monitors if your default browser or email client has been changed and gives you a **1-Click-Fix** to restore your preferences instantly.

---

## Features

- **Vigilant Monitoring** — Monitors your browser and mail settings 24/7 in the background. Detects changes immediately at Login, Wake, or Unlock.
- **1-Click-Fix** — Reset your preferred browser or email client back as the default program with a single click.
- **Browser Support** — Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi, DuckDuckGo, Orion — and many more.
- **Email Clients** — Apple Mail, Outlook, Thunderbird, Betterbird, Spark, Airmail, Canary Mail, Mimestream and many more.
- **Change Log** — Keeps a logfile of all changes to your default browser and email client – including timestamps.
- **Flexible Timers** — Choose between 1min, 5min, 10min, 1h or daily checking.
- **Snooze Mode** — Pause monitoring for 15min, 1h or until tonight.
- **Lightweight** — Only about 9 MB, minimal CPU/RAM usage. Runs invisibly in your menu bar.
- **No cloud, no surveillance** — Everything runs locally on your Mac. No cloud dependency, full control. No subscription.

## Installation

1. Download the ZIP file
2. Unzip & drag `DefaultBrowserGuard.app` to your Applications folder
3. Launch the app and grant permissions

> **Note:** macOS may block the app on first launch because it is not signed by Apple. See our [FAQ](FAQ.md) for instructions on how to open it.
