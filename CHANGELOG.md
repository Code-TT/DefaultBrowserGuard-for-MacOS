## [1.0.0] - 2026-02-28

### Added
- Initial release of DefaultBrowserGuard
- Support for Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera, Vivaldi and more
- Email client monitoring: Apple Mail, Outlook, Thunderbird, Betterbird, Spark, Airmail, Canary Mail, Mimestream
- Real-time monitoring at Login, Wake and Unlock
- 1-Click-Fix for quickly resetting default apps
- Live statistics and change history with timestamps
- Snooze mode: pause for 15 minutes, 1 hour or until tonight
- Flexible timers: 1min, 5min, 10min, 1h or daily check
- German/English support
- 100% local – no cloud, full privacy
